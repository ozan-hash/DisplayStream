# CleanQix – Copy Brief

Hi! Here is the brief for the new CleanQix ads. We need copy for **1 Search campaign (5 ad groups)** and **1 PMax campaign** for Shopping. All copy is in English, for the US market.

## About the product
CleanQix are sanitizing wipes for food contact surfaces. The main point is **no rinse**: you wipe the surface, it dries, and it's ready for food again. No bucket, no mixing, no rinsing.

Our buyers are restaurant owners, kitchen managers and people who buy supplies for foodservice. They are busy, so please keep the copy short, simple and practical. Talk like a kitchen person, not like a chemist.

## Please be careful with claims
Some claims we can only use if the client confirms them. I will send the confirmed list. Until then, please mark these lines:
- NSF listed
- Kills norovirus / flu / 99.9%
- Free shipping (maybe there is a minimum order)

Please don't write "health inspector approved". Inspectors don't approve products, so this is not correct. "Inspection-ready" is OK.

## Search ads (RSA)
For each ad group: **15 headlines (max 30 characters)**, **4 descriptions (max 90)** and **2 paths (max 15)**.
Google mixes the headlines, so each one must work alone. Please don't write the same idea twice with different words.

| Ad group | Who is searching | What to say | Must use |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Brand | People who already know CleanQix | Official store, trust, offer | CleanQix |
| 2. No-Rinse / Food Contact | People looking for the function | No rinse, food safe, saves time | no-rinse, food safe |
| 3. Restaurant & Foodservice | Restaurants, bars, cafeterias, catering | Fast cleaning between guests, busy kitchens | restaurant, commercial |
| 4. Prep Surface & Utensil | Cutting boards, prep tables, utensils | Move from one prep job to the next without the sink | cutting board, prep |
| 5. Compliance | People worried about inspections and safety | Certified, inspection-ready (only confirmed claims) | NSF, HACCP |

Free shipping is not a separate group. Please add it as 1–2 headlines in every group (if confirmed).

**Some examples, just for the tone:**
- No-Rinse Food Contact Wipes
- Wipe It. Done. No Rinse Needed
- Sanitize Tables Between Guests
- Cutting Boards Sanitized Fast
- Built for Busy Restaurants
- Inspection-Ready Kitchens

## PMax (Shopping)
- 15 headlines (max 30), 5 long headlines (max 90), 4 descriptions (max 90), 1 short description (max 60)
- Also a new product title (max 150) and description for the Merchant Center feed
- PMax shows ads on many places (Shopping, YouTube, Display…), so the copy should talk about the product in general, not one keyword
- Please mix all 4 angles: no-rinse, restaurants, prep, compliance

For images we will use the photos from the website. If you have ideas for crops or a short video, tell me.

## How to send it
Please send one Google Sheet: one tab for each ad group and one for PMax. Put the character count next to each line.
