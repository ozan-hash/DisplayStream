# CleanQix: Copywriter Brief
**Scope:** 1 Search campaign (5 ad groups, one RSA each) + 1 Performance Max asset group (Shopping)
**Market:** US, English copy · **Audience:** B2B foodservice buyers (owners, GMs, kitchen managers, purchasing)

---

## 1. Product in one line
CleanQix are **no-rinse sanitizing/disinfectant wipes made for food contact surfaces**: wipe, air-dry, and the surface is ready for food. There's no bucket, no mixing and no rinse step.

**Core promise:** Faster sanitizing between tasks and between guests, and safe on food-contact surfaces.
**Tone:** Confident, practical, operator-to-operator. Short verbs. Talk about the kitchen, not chemistry.

## 2. ⚠️ Claim rules (read first)
Use only the claims below that the client confirms in writing, and match the product label word for word.

| Claim | Status | Note |
|---|---|---|
| No-rinse on food contact surfaces | Confirm | This is the core claim, so it has to be on the label |
| NSF listed / certified | Confirm | Use the exact listing category (e.g. "NSF D2") |
| Kills norovirus / flu / 99.9% | Confirm | Only with EPA registration; name the virus exactly as the label does |
| Free shipping | Confirm | Add a minimum order ("on orders $X+") if there is one |
| "Health inspector approved" | **Do not use** | Inspectors don't approve products, so the claim is misleading. Use "Inspection-ready" instead |
| "HACCP compliant" | Soft | Say "Supports your HACCP plan", not "HACCP approved" |

## 3. Specs
**Search RSA (per ad group):** 15 headlines (max 30 characters), 4 descriptions (max 90), 2 display paths (max 15 each).
- Every headline must work on its own, because Google mixes them freely.
- Headline mix: 3–4 contain the ad group's keyword · 3–4 benefit · 2–3 proof/trust · 2 offer/CTA · 1–2 brand.
- Don't repeat the same idea in different words; Google rates that as low asset strength.
- Mark 2 headlines as *pin candidates* for position 1 (keyword-led).

**PMax (1 asset group):** 15 headlines (max 30) · 5 long headlines (max 90) · 4 descriptions (max 90) + 1 short description (max 60) · business name (max 25) · CTA: *Shop now*.
Also: **product title + description** rewrite for the Merchant Center feed (title max 150; front-load the most important words in the first 70).

## 4. Ad groups

| # | Ad group | Who's searching | Angle / key message | Keyword to echo | Path |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Brand – CleanQix** | Already knows the product | Official store, trust, offer, pack sizes | "CleanQix" | /CleanQix /Wipes |
| 2 | **No-Rinse / Food Contact** | Wants the *functionality* | No rinse, no bucket, food-safe in one step, time saved | "No-Rinse", "Food Safe" | /No-Rinse /Food-Safe |
| 3 | **Restaurant & Foodservice** | *Industry*: restaurants, bars, cafeterias, food trucks, catering | Speed between guests, busy lines, front- and back-of-house | "Restaurant", "Commercial" | /Restaurant /Kitchen |

**Free shipping** isn't a separate ad group. Use it as an offer headline in all 5 RSAs and in PMax (if confirmed).

## 5. PMax direction
- Write for the **product**, not the ad group. PMax serves on Shopping, YouTube, Display, Gmail and Discover, so copy has to make sense with no search query next to it.
- Long headlines: problem → solution ("Stop rinsing prep surfaces. CleanQix sanitizes food contact surfaces in one wipe.")
- Cover all 4 angles across the 15 headlines: functionality, industry, task, compliance.
- Images: use site photos. We need 1.91:1, 1:1 and 4:5 crops, plus a logo (1:1 and 4:1). Priority shots: wipe in use on a prep table or cutting board, a restaurant table between guests, the product on a stainless counter. If possible, add a 10–20s vertical video; without one, Google auto-generates a weak one.

## 6. Deliverable
One sheet: a tab per ad group plus a PMax tab. Show the character count next to every line and flag any line that uses a claim needing confirmation.
